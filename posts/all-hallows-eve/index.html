Halloween is about much more than a costume. It's a chance to be bold, be new, and act a little strange. Today is about being peculiar, so here are some strange halloween facts I googled this morning. The colors of orange and black are intentional to represent the season. Black represents the "death" of summer while orange represents the life of the fall harvest. Trick-or-Treating was once called "guising" in Scotland and Ireland. Kids would dress up in costumes performing songs, poems or other "tricks" in exchange for treats and money. According to this one website, kids used to yell, "Belsnickel!" instead of "Trick-or-Treat" This apparently came from the concept of "belsnickeling," a German-American Christmas tradition where kids dressed in costumes and would have neighbors guess who they were.
