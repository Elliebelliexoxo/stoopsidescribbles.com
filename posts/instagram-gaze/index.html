Instagram Gaze

October 26, 2026
Over the years, i have known a million versions of myself on instagram and it has known a million versions of me. The platform has inadvertently taught me about aesthetics, nostalgia, the romanticism of life through photography, and what it means to really perceive and be perceived. For over a decade, instagram has been my memory log. My instagram knew me when i made my 7th grade cheer team, when I graduated high school, when I moved to New York, when I studied abroad in college, and when I worked in a national park. It has been a way for me to see the linearity of my timeline. To frequently observe, revisit, and at times admire moments of my own life. It is the curation of myself by myself throughout years and years. When i look at those moments, I think about all the ways I've changed, and been so unknowing of what was to come next. As with all things that scale, now on Instagram there is a social rhetoric, an unspoken posting etiquette and perceptions on content, cadence, and follower count. These rules have continued to evolve after the world decided to start mixing business with personal. Today, Instagram is a hub, a pillar, a vessel and machine of the Creator Economy with ad reels, paid promotions, and bots. The cross generational discourse i encounter online regarding social platforms goes on about our instagram selves being more like brands than photo albums. It is a disheartening adjustment. What if I don't want to franchise my identity? Now on Instagram I don't follow celebrities, scroll reels, or see a lot of posts from my friends. Yet I keep the app because my account is comforting. However strange, in a way it must settle an aspect of my own perception of my identity. When I look at my account, I feel a cohesion. Is it an aesthetic? A taste profile? I'm not sure, but there is something pleasant and satisfactory in the timeline and natural image coherence. Im not sure what brought me on here to write about this. Maybe I'm wondering if anyone is relating to me here. Maybe this is a way for me to reclaim my own gaze. I like my instagram photo album and I want to feel more comfortable adding to it than I do.
